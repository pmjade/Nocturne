📖 The Story

Seraphine Ashveil died at seventeen and no one noticed — not right away. She had always been the kind of girl who flickered at the edges of rooms, too quiet to be remembered, too strange to be forgotten.

When the Nocturne comes for a soul, it offers a choice: dissolve into the dark, or become one of its Forlorn — the keepers of grief who walk the boundary between the waking world and the place where sorrow pools like black water.

Seraphine chooses neither. She bargains. And the dark, intrigued, listens.

🌑 Fragments of the Dark

There are those who mourn and move on, and those who mourn until the mourning becomes the shape of them. The Nocturne does not come for the former. It comes for the latter, gently, the way water comes for the shore.

— Chapter I · The Girl Who Flickered

"I am not afraid of you," she said to the dark.
The dark did not answer. It simply waited.
It had learned long ago that bravery and foolishness look identical from a distance.

— Chapter II · The Bargain at the Threshold

A Forlorn is not a ghost. A ghost is what remains when the self refuses to leave. A Forlorn is what happens when the self is given a purpose to stay for.
